# 12: Owners manage who belongs to their School

**What to build:** An Owner invites another person by email address, chooses whether they are an Owner or an Editor, sees the current members and their roles, and removes someone who has left. Editors can do none of this.

This keeps the operator out of the loop every September when a PAC executive turns over.

**Blocked by:** 09

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] An Owner invites a person by email and chooses their role
- [ ] An invited person who is new gets an account on first sign-in
- [ ] An Owner sees the current membership list with roles
- [ ] An Owner removes a member, revoking their access
- [ ] The last Owner of a School cannot be removed or demoted
- [ ] An Editor cannot reach or act on any membership function
